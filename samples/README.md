# Sample Images

All images in this folder come from **four original photos taken by the project author** with a phone camera. No stock photos or images from other sources are stored here.

Fifteen files are listed because most originals have rotated copies (`-rot90`, `-rot180`, `-rot270`). They are the same photo turned, not separate scenes, so they are useful for demos and for checking that sorting and detection handle orientation, but not as independent test data.

## Originals

| File | Subject |
|---|---|
| `fridge-salad-drawer.jpg` | Salad drawer of a fridge: leaves, carrots, bananas, tomatoes |
| `potatoes-in-pan.jpg` | Boiled potatoes in a pan |
| `freezer-chicken.jpg` | Freezer drawer with chicken breasts and frozen vegetables |
| `freezer-blurry.jpg` | Deliberately kept blurry freezer shot, to see how detection behaves on a poor photo |

## Preparation

- Photos were shrunk to 1400 px on the long side, and camera metadata was removed.
- Brand names, logos, barcodes and printed labels on packaging were pixelated, and the camera's corner stamp was cropped off. This is why some areas look blocky.
- Please check any new photo for visible brands, logos or people before adding it here.
